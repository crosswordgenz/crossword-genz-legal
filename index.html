Privacy Policy

Crossword Challenge Gen Z · Effective date: October 15, 2026 · Last updated: October 15, 2026

Short version: your game progress stays on your phone. We do not run our own servers and we never sell your data. Some data is processed by Google (Firebase, AdMob) and Unity to run leaderboards, analytics, crash reports and ads.

1. Who we are

Crossword Challenge Gen Z ("the App") is developed and published by Paweł Grzejszczak, an independent developer based in Poland ("we", "us"). We are the data controller for the purposes of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Contact: crosswordgenz.support@gmail.com

2. Data stored only on your device

Game progress, solved words, level and XP, coin balance, unlocked avatars, frames and banners, settings and daily streaks are stored locally on your device. This data is not sent to us. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data permanently deletes it and we cannot restore it.

We do not collect your name, email, precise location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone input, or health data. The App does not use AI to process personal data.

4. Legal bases (GDPR)

  • Consent – personalized ads and analytics, where required. On iOS you choose this in the App Tracking Transparency prompt; ads may still be shown, but non-personalized.
  • Legitimate interest – crash reporting, security and keeping the leaderboard fair.
  • Performance of a contract – providing the game and in-app purchases you request.

5. International transfers

Google and Unity may process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States, under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and/or Standard Contractual Clauses.

6. Retention

Leaderboard entries are kept while the leaderboard exists or until you ask us to delete them. Analytics and crash data are kept according to Google's default retention periods (up to 14 months for analytics, 90 days for crash reports).

7. Children

The App is intended for users aged 13 and over. It is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you are under 16 (or the age of digital consent in your country), please use the App with the consent of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child under 13 has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act), you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. We do not sell or share personal data for cross-context behavioral advertising beyond the ad services listed above. You can reset or limit your Advertising ID in your device settings. EU users may lodge a complaint with their local data protection authority (in Poland: UODO, uodo.gov.pl).

9. Deleting your leaderboard data

Email crosswordgenz.support@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request" and your in-game nickname. We will respond within 30 days.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
