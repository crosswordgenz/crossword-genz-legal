Support

Crossword Challenge Gen Z · Developer: Paweł Grzejszczak (crosswordgenz)

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea for a new category? Email us at crosswordgenz.support@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 business days.

To help us solve your problem faster, please include your in-game nickname, your device model and OS version, and a short description of what happened.

Frequently asked questions

I paid for coins but they never arrived

First, close and reopen the app — purchases are confirmed when the game starts. If the coins are still missing, email us with your nickname and the receipt from Apple or Google, and we will add them to your account manually.

I bought Premium and it disappeared

Premium is a one-time purchase tied to your App Store or Google Play account. On iOS, open the Shop and tap Restore Purchases. On Android it is restored automatically when you sign in with the same Google account. If it does not come back, email us.

My progress is gone after reinstalling

Game progress (solved words, level, coins, unlocked items) is stored on your device only, so uninstalling the app deletes it. Your leaderboard entry stays linked to your nickname.

A rewarded ad will not load

Rewarded ads need an internet connection and are not always available in every region. Wait a minute and try again. If an ad fails, no coins are deducted and nothing is charged.

Why can't I use a nickname?

Nicknames on the leaderboard are unique, so someone may already be using it. Pick another one — you can change it later in your profile.

How do I report offensive content?

Email us with the nickname or the clue in question and we will review it.
